Pro Intros

Ten intros, each built on one clear idea.

P01

Momentum

A single ink stroke (the swoosh, secretly carrying the other two strokes inside it) whips round the frame on one big arc, lands, and its momentum flicks the body and then the arm out of it into place; the word rises in a wave.

open full size

P02

Macro

We open on an extreme close-up of the swoosh's curved edge, levelled like a horizon (ink above, white below); one continuous pull-out with the roll unwinding reveals it is the mark, then the word slides out from behind it.

open full size

P03

Collapse

The frame is one sheet of ink on a white table; it folds over onto itself flap by flap, in real perspective, until it is exactly the size of the mark; the white each fold uncovers carries the letters in, then the block taps down and the offcuts around the mark drop away.

open full size

P04

Giant type

Huge cropped letters t, a, p fly past the lens as bold black-and-white crops; the camera pulls back as they settle into the word, then the mark swoops in last and taps down into its slot.

open full size

P05

Pivot lock

The mark's three strokes turn as a three-blade rotor about the corner where they meet; it indexes like a precision mechanism and leaves each stroke at its own angle with a hard click, then the word telescopes out of the mark.

open full size

P06v2

Ink flood

One pen draws the mark's outline in a single fast hairline, closes the loop and taps the body: ink floods through the three strokes like liquid in a channel, then the letters are outlined and flood the same way, left to right.

open full size · first version

P07v2

Editorial cuts

Four extreme crops cut on eighth notes spell the word in reading order (mark, t, a, p) as the logo glides past; the last cut opens to the wide, the glide brakes and the letters close up behind the mark one after another.

open full size · first version

P08v2

Wiper

The mark sweeps across the empty frame from right to left on a long wiper-blade arc; the word appears exactly behind its trailing edge as it passes, and the mark brakes into a firm stop, tips forward on its base and rocks back upright.

open full size · first version

P09

Magnet

The mark drops in and turns into a magnet: the letters drift in from off-frame, swing into line like compass needles, speed up as they get closer and snap into place one by one, and each snap ripples back through the chain.

open full size

P10

Depth stack

The logo starts as flat paper sheets floating at different depths, seen in real perspective from above and to the side; the camera moves and they shift against each other, then the stack slams flat onto one plane on the beat and the logo turns to face you.

Vertical launch screens for the app.

Launch

Press

Loader

Home hand-off

Machined

Global Brands

5

Inspired by how the biggest brands animate: sound logos, patterns, energy.

B01v2

Mnemonic

A sonic logo you can see: a live audio meter plays a three-note mnemonic; each note bursts in the shape of one stroke and leaves it behind in ink, the closing chord writes 'tap', and the striped logo snaps solid on the click.

open full size · first version

B02v2

Mosaic

The frame opens on a brand wallpaper of small Tap marks ticking on the beat; a wave clears it from the centre out and the logo is left standing, made of the pattern, then the marks swell into each other and lock solid.

open full size · first version

B03v2

Strike

Sports-ad energy: bold rows of 'tap' race across a tilted frame, time ramps into slow motion as the mark tumbles in, then it strikes dead centre on one inverted impact frame, the rows blow off the screen and the word slams home letter by letter.

open full size · first version

B04

Stripe rush

We fly through a stream of crisp ink stripes; the last stripes to arrive are the mark itself, cut into bands, which slam into place and pull their tails in until the mark is whole; then the word streaks in on the same lanes.

open full size

B05

Grid system

The logo is cut on a square grid and the whole frame becomes a pattern made from its own curves; the grid turns on the beat like a living identity system, then everything but the mark switches off, the mark's modules turn true, the gutters close, and the word types in on the same grid.

open full size

Built on the 12 principles of animation: the mark acts through motion.

D01

Wind-up

The mark, close and alone, leans back into a long slow wind-up, holds the coil, then snaps forward in four frames and flings its letters out: t and a arc into place, p is lobbed clean out of shot and drops in last.

open full size

D02

Swing

The whole logo swings in as one chain on a big pendulum arc, the mark leading and its name trailing like a kite tail; each piece rises into its place a beat after the one before and settles.

open full size

D03

Peek

The word sits alone; the mark peeks up out of the floor beside it, leans over to look at the word, glances back at us, ducks out of sight, then springs up and drops into its place, pleased.

open full size

D04

Reaction

The word sits alone; the mark leaps in, hangs at the top of its arc and drops into place with a heavy thud, and the impact kicks t, a and p up one after another before they rock back flat.

open full size

D05

Button taps

The word starts switched off (grey); the mark hops along and taps each letter like a key: it presses down, clicks on in ink and pops back up. The p, pressed hardest, springboards the mark into a backflip home.

Pencil

Smear

T03

Cut-Out

Multiplane

Flipbook
